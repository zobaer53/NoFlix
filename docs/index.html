An ad-free way to stream movies and TV shows

Everything you need to find something to watch, and nothing you don't.

Built-in ad blocker

AdGuard and EasyPrivacy filter lists with a native ad-block engine stop ads, pop-ups, redirects and crypto-miners.

Movies & TV series

Trending, popular, top-rated, now playing and upcoming lists, powered by The Movie Database (TMDB).

Full-screen player

One tap on Play opens the video in landscape full screen. The rest of the app stays in portrait.

Search & filters

Find any movie or TV show by title, or browse by genre, year and rating.

Watchlist

Save movies and series to watch later, all kept on your device.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up and no analytics. Lists are cached so you can browse offline.

Subtitles

Pick a built-in subtitle track, or upload your own .srt or .vtt file from your phone.

Resume where you left off

TV shows reopen at the last episode you watched, and the details screen shows how much is left.

Trailers

Watch the YouTube trailer right from the details screen before you press Play.

Screenshots

A clean, modern interface built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3.

How to install NoFlix

NoFlix isn't on the Play Store. Install the signed APK from GitHub in under a minute.

  1. Download the APK
    Get NoFlix.apk on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer).
  2. Allow the install
    Open the file and allow Install unknown apps for your browser if Android asks.
  3. Press Play
    Open NoFlix, pick a movie or TV series and tap Play. No ads, no pop-ups.

Updating? Install the new APK over the old one; your watchlist is kept. Coming from 2.0.2 or older? Uninstall that first, because newer releases are signed with a new key.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about NoFlix.

Is NoFlix free?

Yes. NoFlix is completely free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, with no ads or in-app purchases.

How do I watch movies without ads on Android?

Install NoFlix, search for a movie or TV show and tap Play. The built-in ad blocker filters every request the player makes, so ads and pop-ups never load.

Do I need an account?

No. NoFlix works without sign-up or login.

Is NoFlix on the Google Play Store?

No. Download the APK from GitHub Releases.

Can I use my own subtitles?

Yes. In the player, open the subtitles menu, tap Upload .srt / .vtt and pick the file from your phone. Built-in subtitle tracks are listed in the same menu.

Does NoFlix host any videos?

No. NoFlix does not host, upload or store any video files. Movie and TV information comes from the TMDB API.
